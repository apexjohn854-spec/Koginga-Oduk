About Us

Miranga Seventh-day Adventist Church is located in Miranga, Kenya. We are part of the worldwide Seventh-day Adventist family. Our mission is to share the everlasting gospel and prepare people for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

Our Beliefs: Sabbath observance, Second Coming of Christ, Bible as foundation, Healthful living, Community service.
